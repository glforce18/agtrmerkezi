SECTION 6 – DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
========================================================================
Principles:
1. Clarity over decoration
2. Space equals quality
3. One primary action per page
4. Gaming UI, not corporate UI
5. Subtle motion only

Target feeling:
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Competitive
- Engaging

========================================================================
SECTION 7 – VISUAL HIERARCHY
========================================================================
Each page must define ONE primary action.

Homepage primary CTA:
- "Forum'a Katıl" OR "Sunucuları Keşfet"

Primary CTA rules:
- Larger size
- Solid fill
- Bright orange
- Subtle glow

Secondary actions:
- Outline buttons
- Reduced contrast

========================================================================
SECTION 8 – TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM
========================================================================
Fonts:
- Headings: Inter / Poppins / Sora
- Body: Inter / Roboto

Scale:
H1: 48–56px
H2: 24–28px
H3: 16–18px
Body: 14–15px
Meta: 12–13px

Line-height:
- Body: 1.5–1.7
- Headings: 1.2–1.3

========================================================================
SECTION 9 – GRID & SPACING
========================================================================
- Page padding: 80–120px
- Card gap: ≥24px
- Card padding: 20–28px

Whitespace is mandatory.

========================================================================
SECTION 10 – CARD SYSTEM
========================================================================
Cards used for:
- Forum
- Stats
- Servers
- Jackpot

Style:
- Radius: 12–16px
- Slightly lighter background
- Subtle border

Hover:
translateY(-4px)
glow shadow
transition 0.2s

========================================================================
SECTION 11 – COLOR ROLE SYSTEM
========================================================================
Orange = primary emphasis only.

Roles:
- Primary (Armor, Jackpot, CTA): Orange
- Success (TL, wins): Green
- Info (stats): Blue/Purple
- Warning: Yellow
- Error: Red
- Neutral: Gray

========================================================================
SECTION 12 – NAVBAR & HEADER
========================================================================
- Active page indicator
- Hover transitions
- Avatar borders by role

========================================================================
SECTION 13 – JACKPOT EXPERIENCE (CRITICAL)
========================================================================
Goal:
Dramatic, memorable win experience.

Requirements:
- Large spinner
- Background blur/vignette
- Noise reduction during spin

Win moment:
- Screen dim
- Winner centered
- Armor emphasized
- Glow/burst animation

========================================================================
SECTION 14 – MICRO-INTERACTIONS
========================================================================
- Hover feedback
- Click feedback
- HOT pulse badges
- Smooth transitions
- Custom scrollbar

Avoid over-animation.

========================================================================
SECTION 15 – UI STATES
========================================================================
All interactive elements must define:
- Loading (prefer skeletons)
- Empty
- Error
- Success

No silent states allowed.

========================================================================
SECTION 16 – EMPTY STATES
========================================================================
Empty states must be intentional.

Examples:
- No forum threads
- Jackpot waiting
- No servers

Empty state must not resemble a bug.

========================================================================
SECTION 17 – COMPONENT REUSABILITY
========================================================================
Mandatory reusable components:
- Buttons
- Cards
- Badges
- Modals
- Tooltips

Avoid one-off styles.

========================================================================
SECTION 18 – RESPONSIVE DESIGN
========================================================================
Breakpoints:
- Desktop: 1200px+
- Tablet: 768–1199px
- Mobile: <768px

Rules:
- Maintain hierarchy
- Reduce density
- Stack cards
- Keep primary CTA visible

SECTION 19 – PERFORMANCE RULES
========================================================================

- Transform & opacity only
- 150–300ms duration
- No infinite loops (except indicators)

Performance > visuals.
